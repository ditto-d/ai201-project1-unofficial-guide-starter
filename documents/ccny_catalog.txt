CCNY Computer Science Undergraduate Curriculum — Bachelor of Science

Computer Science deals with information: its efficient representation and transformation; its communication and security; its storage, retrieval, analysis and display. Computer scientists must acquire expertise in the core areas: theory of computation, algorithms and data structures, programming methodology and languages, communications and security, and computer systems and architecture. The Department of Computer Science, established in 1968, offers a broad curriculum.

Program Outcomes: Upon graduation, students are expected to have an ability to analyze a complex computing problem and apply principles of computing to identify solutions. An ability to design, implement, and evaluate a computing-based solution. An ability to communicate effectively in professional contexts. An ability to recognize professional responsibilities and make informed judgments based on legal and ethical principles. An ability to function effectively as a member or leader of a team.

Degree Requirements: Earn at least 127 credits. Earn at least 30 credits at The City College. Earn a minimum GPA of 2.0.

Math Requirements: Calculus I (MATH 20100), Calculus II (MATH 21200 or 20200), Calculus III (MATH 21300 or 20300), Elements of Linear Algebra (MATH 34600). Minimum grade of C required in all math courses.

Science Requirements: At least 12 credits of Science. Options include Biology, Chemistry, or University Physics I and II. Minimum grade of C required.

Core CS Courses Required: CSC 10300 Introduction to Computing for Majors, CSC 10400 Discrete Mathematical Structures I, CSC 11300 Programming Languages, CSC 21100 Fundamentals of Computer Systems, CSC 21200 Data Structures, CSC 21700 Probability and Statistics for CS, CSC 22000 Algorithms, CSC 22100 Software Design Laboratory, CSC 30100 Numerical Issues in Scientific Programming, CSC 30400 Introduction to Theoretical Computer Science, CSC 32200 Software Engineering, CSC 33200 Operating Systems, CSC 33500 Programming Language Paradigms, CSC 33600 Introduction to Database Systems, CSC 34200 Computer Organization, CSC 34300 Computer Systems Design Laboratory, CSC 36000 Modern Distributed Computing, CSC 38000 Computer Security, CSC 59866 Senior Project I, CSC 59867 Senior Project II.

Elective Groups: Theory and Applications (choose one from: Computability, Formal Languages and Automata, Artificial Intelligence, Combinatorics and Graph Theory, Cryptography, Computational Complexity). Computational Techniques (choose one from: Numerical Analysis, Systems Simulation, Mathematical Optimization, Image Processing, Computer Vision, Computer Graphics). Computer Systems (choose one from: Internet Programming, Computer Networks, Compiler Construction, Distributed Computing, Concurrency in Operating Systems, Real-Time Computing, Web Security).

Recommended four-year sequence: Semester 1 (16-17 credits): Freshman Composition, Calculus I, Introduction to Computing for Majors, Speech. Semester 2 (15-16 credits): Discrete Mathematical Structures I, Programming Languages, Calculus II. Semester 3 (16 credits): Fundamentals of Computer Systems, Data Structures, Probability and Statistics, Calculus III, Writing for Engineering. Semester 4 (15 credits): Algorithms, Software Design Laboratory, Engineering Economics, Linear Algebra. Semester 5 (17 credits): Numerical Issues in Scientific Programming, Theoretical Computer Science, Software Engineering, Programming Language Paradigms. Semester 6 (17 credits): Operating Systems, Database Systems, Computer Organization, Computer Systems Design Laboratory. Semester 7 (15 credits): Senior Project I, two CS electives, Technical Elective. Semester 8 (15 credits): Senior Project II, CS Elective, Technical Elective, Free Electives.

Co-Op Study option available for students in good standing with GPA above threshold. Students may substitute co-op credits for technical and free electives.
