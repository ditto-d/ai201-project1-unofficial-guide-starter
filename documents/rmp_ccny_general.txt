General observations from student reviews across CCNY CS professors:
Students most commonly praise professors who give clear explanations, provide fair and curved exams, keep organized course materials, and are accessible outside class hours. Negative reviews most often cite disorganization, lectures that do not match exam content, very harsh grading with no partial credit, and professors who seem uninterested in student success. Many students note that the quality of instruction varies dramatically between professors teaching the same course. Students consistently recommend checking RateMyProfessor before registering for any section.

The gap between good and bad professors in the CS department is very wide. Some professors are genuinely inspiring and will change how you think about computer science. Others are extremely difficult to follow and the course becomes largely self-taught. Knowing which professor to choose for each course is one of the most important decisions a CS student at CCNY makes. Students share this information informally through Discord servers, class group chats, and word of mouth among upperclassmen.
